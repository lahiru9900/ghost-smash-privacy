Privacy Policy — Ghost Smash

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Ghost Smash ("the app") is an offline arcade game. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.

Information stored on your device

To work properly the app saves a small amount of game data only on your device:

  • your high score, selected difficulty and sound setting
  • your level progress, stars and in-game coins
  • which how-to-play guides you have already seen

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app uses only the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback while you play. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or the internet.

Children

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links to other apps or websites and no way to communicate with other people.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same web address and the date above will be updated.
